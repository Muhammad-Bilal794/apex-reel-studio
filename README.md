# Apex Reels Studio

Build a sleek, high-end portfolio and client-acquisition landing page for an elite Video Editor & Short-Form Content Agency. The design must look cinematic, premium, and hyper-focused on video production aesthetics (similar to top Creator/Agency sites).

### Visual Style & Theme

- Color Palette: Deep cinematic dark mode (primary: #0a0a0c, secondary cards: #121318, subtle borders: #222530), glowing neon accents (amber/orange #ff6b00 or electric cyan #00e5ff highlights), clean white typography.

- Feel: High FPS, modern motion graphics studio, glassmorphism, glowing gradient halos behind cards, ultra-crisp borders (1px border-slate-800).

- Animations: Framer Motion-style micro-interactions, subtle hover tilt/glow on media cards, smooth scroll-triggered fades.

### Key Sections to Include

1. Sticky Cinematic Navbar:

   - Logo ("VFX Studio" / "Apex Media"), Links (Showreel, Work, Process, Pricing/Contact), and a glowing CTA button ("Book an Edit").

2. Hero Section:

   - High-impact punchy typography: "Turn Views Into Revenue With High-Retention Short-Form Edits".

   - Subtitle focusing on pacing, sound design, and viral retention.

   - Primary CTA: "Watch Showreel" + Secondary CTA: "Work With Us".

   - Central Feature: Large cinematic video preview card with a custom glowing play button overlay and soundwave animations.

3. Social Proof / Stat Ticker:

   - Metric pill badges: "100M+ Views Generated", "4.9/5 Client Rating", "70+ Creators Scaled".

   - Client brand logos scrolling ticker.

4. 9:16 Portrait Reel Showcase (THE CORE FOCUS):

   - A dedicated vertical video grid mimicking TikTok/Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts (aspect-ratio: 9/16).

   - Display 4-6 vertical card slots featuring high-contrast thumbnails, title overlay, play badge, and category tags (e.g., "Talking Head", "3D Motion", "Podcast Cut", "Vlog Retention").

   - Interactive Behavior: Clicking any card opens a seamless dark-backdrop modal playing the vertical video in crisp 9:16 format with play/pause and mute controls.

   - Hover effect: Card scales up slightly (scale-105), border lights up with a subtle glow, and subtle shimmer across the thumbnail.

5. Editing Workflow & Craft Breakdown:

   - Interactive 3-4 step process ("The Retention Formula"):

     - 01. Hook & Pacing Optimization

     - 02. Sound Design & Foley (SFX visual waveforms)

     - 03. Custom Dynamic Graphics & B-Roll Tracking

     - 04. 4K Master & Platform Delivery

6. Interactive Before & After / Editing Timeline Showcase:

   - A mockup card displaying video editing timeline tracks (VFX, SFX, Audio layers) or an interactive before/after slider showing raw footage vs. color-graded & animated final export.

7. Client Testimonials & Social Proof:

   - Creator avatars with quote cards, channel follower counts, and view metrics.

8. Direct Contact & Booking Footer:

   - Clean calendar booking embed placeholder or contact form with custom inputs (Full Name, Channel Link, Project Scope, Monthly Budget).

   - Social links and copyright.

### Details & Responsiveness

- Fully responsive across mobile (single column reels slider) and desktop (multi-column 9:16 showcase).

- Use Lucide icons, Tailwind CSS, and polished typography hierarchy (Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans).
